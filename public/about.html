About Elimika Tanzania

Empowering Primary (Standard 1–7), Ordinary Secondary (Form 1–4), and Advanced Secondary (Form 5–6) Education

Elimika Tanzania (https://www.elimika.online) is a comprehensive digital learning library, interactive 3D science laboratory, and teacher productivity studio built for students, teachers, and parents across the United Republic of Tanzania.

Our Mission

Our mission is to ensure every Tanzanian learner and educator has instant, zero-barrier access to official Tanzania Institute of Education (TIE) curriculum textbooks, national syllabuses, NECTA past examination papers, worked marking schemes, interactive 3D ESTEAM simulations, and competency-based teacher tools on any smartphone, tablet, or computer.

What Elimika Tanzania Provides

NECTA Past Papers & Solutions

Complete SFNA, PSLE, FTNA, CSEE, and ACSEE past examination papers paired with worked step-by-step marking schemes.

Official TIE Books & Syllabuses

142+ competency-based TIE student textbooks and official Ministry of Education syllabuses across Primary, O-Level, and A-Level.

3D Interactive Science Simulations

Hands-on 3D virtual labs for Chemistry (Titration), Physics (Ohm's Law, Simple Pendulum), Biology (Human Heart, Photosynthesis, Osmosis, Mitosis), and Mathematics (Quadratics, Trigonometry).

Teacher Studio & Moha AI

Instant 4-Stage TIE Lesson Plans, 12-Column PO-RALG Schemes of Work, Daily 20-Question Quizzes, and the curriculum-trained Moha AI Tutor.

Get in Touch

Have a question, school partnership inquiry, or missing past paper request? Visit our dedicated Contact Us page to reach our editorial team in Dar es Salaam.
