Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026  ·  Last Updated: November 21, 2026  ·  Android Package IDs: online.elimika.twa / online.elimika.tanzania

At Elimika Tanzania (https://www.elimika.online), including our web platform and our official Google Play Android application (Elimika TZ), we respect and protect the privacy of all students, teachers, and parents using our educational platform. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you access our Past Papers, TIE Textbooks, Official Syllabuses, 3D Science Simulations, Educational Games, and Teacher Productivity Tools.

1. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

  • No Mandatory Account Registration for Core Study Resources: Students and teachers can read past examination papers, marking schemes, TIE textbooks, syllabuses, and interactive 3D science simulations without creating a mandatory user account or submitting personal financial information.
  • Optional User-Provided Inputs: When you voluntarily use interactive features—such as entering a display nickname for a daily quiz challenge, typing a study question in the Moha AI Tutor, or filling in school/teacher header fields to generate a printable TIE Lesson Plan or Scheme of Work—that information is processed solely to deliver the requested educational output.
  • Camera & Microphone Permissions (Optional): If you choose to upload or photograph a textbook page for question extraction or use voice input for search/tutor prompts, the app only accesses your camera, photo picker, or microphone when explicitly initiated by you. Media is transmitted securely over HTTPS for processing and is never sold or shared with data brokers.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Elimika Tanzania uses your device's browser storage (localStorage and Service Worker cache) to store:

  • Your visual theme preference (Light Mode, Dark Mode, or System Default).
  • Your selected class level (Standard 1–7 or Form 1–6) and recent subject preferences.
  • Your quiz progress, game high scores, and offline print basket.

This preference data remains on your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser/app storage data.

3. Third-Party Services, Google AdSense & Cookies

  • Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookie (Publisher ID: ca-pub-7814843623545511): Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to https://www.elimika.online or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve relevant educational and general advertisements. Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.
  • AI Processing & Cloud Infrastructure: Interactive AI tutor queries and shared quiz challenges are processed securely over encrypted HTTPS (TLS) connections via Google Cloud / Firebase and Google Gemini API infrastructure strictly to generate curriculum-aligned responses.

4. Data Sharing, Retention & Deletion

  • No Sale of Personal Data: We never sell, rent, or trade student or teacher personal information to third parties.
  • Data Retention & Deletion Requests: Local study data stored on your device can be deleted immediately by clearing the app's storage or browser cache. If you wish to request deletion of any shared quiz challenge entry or inquiry submitted to our support desk, please contact us using the details in Section 6 below and we will permanently delete it within 7 business days.

5. Children's & Student Privacy

Elimika Tanzania provides general primary and secondary curriculum resources aligned with Tanzania Institute of Education (TIE) and NECTA syllabuses. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data from children. Parents or guardians who believe a child has submitted personal information may contact us directly for immediate removal.
